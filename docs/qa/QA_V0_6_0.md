## Required real-device acceptance

Test the following before calling V0.6.0 production accepted:

1. Open **The Golden Path** from Home; the old Coming Soon toast must not appear.
2. Verify all six focuses in English and Thai.
3. Select a focus, leave before completion, reopen — the day must remain unconsumed.
4. Start a reading; verify the full compact 78-card deck is available.
5. Select exactly three cards; no duplicate card may appear.
6. After card 3/3, the deck stage must collapse with no giant dead space.
7. Reveal; verify positions are Where You Stand / What Blocks / The Way Forward.
8. Confirm the three position texts clearly address the selected focus and relate to one another.
9. Confirm Your Path at a Glance, Your Golden Path, three next steps, and reflection question all read as one consultation.
10. Tap each of the three artworks; confirm the correct card enlarges at approximately single-card reading scale.
11. Close artwork viewer with × and backdrop; reading position remains stable.
12. Save Image and Share; verify the Golden Path title, focus, three cards, and synthesis are correct.
13. Close the mode after completion and reopen on the same local day; verify the same focus, same cards, and same reading conclusion return automatically.
14. Verify Quiet Countdown is visible after completion and counts toward the next device-local day.
15. Change language after restore; verify native TH/EN content remains semantically consistent and the card selection does not change.
16. Verify Three-Card Reading still has its magnifier and daily lock.
17. Verify Daily Guidance and Ask Ganesha still open normally.
18. Verify music behavior remains unchanged during open/close, viewer use, app backgrounding, and foreground return.
19. Verify Settings shows BUILD 0.6.0.
20. After deployment refresh/relaunch, verify the PWA is serving V0.6.0 rather than a mixed cache.

## Honesty gate

Static/package QA passing is **not** real-device acceptance. Do not label V0.6.0 Android, iOS, or production PASS until those checks have actually been performed on the relevant device/platform.
