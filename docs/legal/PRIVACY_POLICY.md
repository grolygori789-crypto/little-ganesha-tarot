## English

### Privacy Policy

*What stays on your device, what can leave it, and the controls you have.*

#### 1. Privacy approach

Little Ganesha Tarot is designed as a local-first application. In the current release there is no user account, cloud Journal, advertising SDK, behavioural advertising system or product analytics SDK operated by Benedict Interactive.

This Policy describes the application as it exists in this release. If a future feature materially changes how personal data is handled, the Policy will be updated before or when that feature is introduced, and consent will be requested where applicable law requires it.

#### 2. Information you choose to store locally

Optional profile information such as your display name and date of birth is stored in browser/device storage. Reading preferences, language, motion/audio settings, daily reading state, and the accepted legal-version/timestamp used to remember your acknowledgement are also stored locally so the app can behave consistently on your device.

These local records are used by the app on your device. Benedict Interactive does not intentionally receive them through an application backend in the current release.

#### 3. Ask Ganesha privacy

Ask Ganesha uses your question on the device to create the reading context and to preserve same-day semantic consistency. Its reading store keeps a fingerprint and structured semantic descriptors together with reading state; it does not store the full exact question text in that reading store.

Your exact Ask Ganesha wording can be saved in the Journal only if you turn on “Save exact Ask Ganesha questions.” That option is OFF by default.

#### 4. Journal and reflections

The Journal is stored locally in IndexedDB on your device. It may contain saved reading snapshots, card identifiers, Lucky Numbers, focus information, your reflections, bookmarks and—only when you enable the relevant option—the exact wording of Ask Ganesha questions.

Journal auto-save can be controlled in Settings. You can delete one entry, delete multiple selected entries, or clear Journal data. Deleting a Journal copy does not reset the original same-day tarot or Lucky Numbers state.

#### 5. Local storage is not cloud backup

Local browser/device storage can be lost if you clear site data, uninstall or reset the app, change browser or device, use storage-cleaning/privacy tools, run out of storage, or experience device failure. The current release does not provide an account-based recovery service or cloud backup for the Journal.

Local app data is not separately encrypted by Little Ganesha Tarot beyond protections provided by your browser, operating system and device. Use an appropriate device lock and avoid storing information you would not want another person with access to your unlocked device to see.

#### 6. Save, Share and screenshots

Save and Share are user-initiated actions. Saving can create an image on your device. Sharing can pass an exported image or text to your operating system, browser, share sheet or a third-party destination you select. Once you choose an external destination, that provider’s privacy practices apply.

The app does not technically block ordinary screenshots. A screenshot is created and handled by your operating system/device. Personal, non-commercial screenshots are permitted under the Terms, but this does not grant broader rights to redistribute protected assets.

#### 7. Voluntary support and PromptPay

The PromptPay support screen displays a QR code and recipient-identification information so you can initiate a voluntary payment in your own banking application. Little Ganesha Tarot does not ask for or receive your banking password, PIN, one-time password, card data or bank login credentials.

The payment is processed outside the app by your bank/payment infrastructure. The current app does not independently receive or verify payment confirmation. Always verify the recipient name and amount in your banking app before authorising a transfer.

#### 8. Hosting, fonts and ordinary connection data

Although personal reading/profile/Journal data is local-first, using the web/PWA can still create ordinary network requests. The hosting provider and network intermediaries may receive standard technical request data such as IP address, timestamps, requested files and browser/device information as part of delivering the app.

The current interface also requests fonts from Google Fonts infrastructure. Google or its service providers may receive ordinary connection/request metadata under their own privacy terms. Benedict Interactive does not use those requests to build a behavioural profile of you.

#### 9. Analytics, advertising and sale of personal data

The current release contains no Benedict Interactive behavioural-advertising SDK and no product analytics SDK. Benedict Interactive does not sell personal profile, Journal or reading data to advertisers in this release.

If analytics, cloud sync, accounts, crash reporting or another remote-data feature is introduced later, this Policy must be updated to describe the data flow before or when the feature becomes active.

#### 10. Retention and deletion

Functional reading records generally persist only for the period required by the applicable daily-reading rule or until browser storage is removed, while profile/preferences and Journal entries can remain locally until you change or delete them. Exact retention can also depend on browser and operating-system storage policies.

Use the controls in Settings and Journal to clear data that the app exposes for deletion. Clearing browser/site storage at the operating-system or browser level can remove additional local app data.

#### 11. Security and sensible use

The app is designed to minimise remote collection, but no device, browser, storage system or network can be promised to be absolutely secure. Keep your device and browser updated, protect access to your device, and avoid entering passwords, financial credentials, government identifiers, medical records or other highly sensitive secrets into tarot questions or reflections.

#### 12. Children and legal capacity

Little Ganesha Tarot is not designed to solicit children’s personal data. If you are not legally able to consent to data handling or enter into the applicable Terms in your jurisdiction, use the app only with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian where required by law.

Because the current product is local-first and has no account-age verification service, a date of birth entered in the optional profile is not used as a legal age-verification mechanism.

#### 13. International use and mandatory rights

The app can be used from multiple countries. Privacy, consumer and data-protection rights can vary by jurisdiction. Nothing in this Policy is intended to remove a right that applicable law makes non-waivable.

#### 14. Changes and contact

This Policy displays its legal version and update date. Material changes to remote collection, sharing or processing will be reflected in an updated policy, with additional notice or consent where required by law.

Privacy, licensing or legal questions may be submitted through the official Benedict Interactive contact channel when available. No unofficial social account should be treated as a legal contact unless Benedict Interactive identifies it as such.

ในรุ่นปัจจุบัน ข้อมูลเหล่านี้ถูกใช้โดยแอปบนอุปกรณ์ของคุณ และ Benedict Interactive ไม่ได้ตั้งใจรับข้อมูลดังกล่าวผ่าน backend ของแอป

ข้อความคำถามจริงจะถูกเก็บใน Journal ก็ต่อเมื่อคุณเปิดตัวเลือก “เก็บข้อความคำถามจริงจากถามพระพิฆเนศน้อย” ซึ่งปิดไว้เป็นค่าเริ่มต้น

#### 1. Privacy approach

Little Ganesha Tarot local-first app के रूप में बनाया गया है। Current release में Benedict Interactive द्वारा चलाया गया user account, cloud Journal, advertising SDK, behavioural advertising system या product analytics SDK नहीं है।

यह Policy current release की वास्तविक data handling को बताती है। Future feature अगर personal-data flow materially बदलता है, तो feature के शुरू होने से पहले/साथ Policy update होगी और जहाँ कानून माँगे वहाँ consent लिया जाएगा।

#### 2. आपके डिवाइस पर वैकल्पिक local information

Display name और date of birth जैसी optional profile information browser/device storage में रहती है। Language, motion/audio preferences, daily reading state और accepted legal version/timestamp भी app continuity तथा acknowledgement याद रखने के लिए locally store होते हैं।

Current release में ये local records आपके डिवाइस पर app द्वारा इस्तेमाल होते हैं; Benedict Interactive application backend के जरिए इन्हें जानबूझकर receive नहीं करता।

#### 3. Ask Ganesha privacy

Ask Ganesha आपके प्रश्न को डिवाइस पर reading context बनाने और same-day semantic consistency रखने के लिए उपयोग करता है। Reading store fingerprint, structured semantic descriptors और reading state रखता है; उसमें पूरा exact question text store नहीं होता।

Exact Ask Ganesha wording Journal में केवल तभी save होता है जब आप “Save exact Ask Ganesha questions” विकल्प चालू करते हैं। यह default रूप से OFF है।

#### 4. Journal और reflections

Journal आपके डिवाइस के IndexedDB में locally store होता है। इसमें saved reading snapshots, card identifiers, Lucky Numbers, focus, reflections, bookmarks और—केवल संबंधित विकल्प चालू होने पर—Ask Ganesha का exact question शामिल हो सकता है।

Settings में auto-save control उपलब्ध है। आप एक entry, कई selected entries या पूरा Journal data delete कर सकते हैं। Journal copy delete करने से original same-day tarot/Lucky Numbers state reset नहीं होता।

#### 5. Local storage cloud backup नहीं है

Site data clear करने, app uninstall/reset, browser/device बदलने, privacy/cleanup tools, storage pressure या device failure से local information खो सकती है। Current release में Journal के लिए account-based recovery या cloud backup नहीं है।

Little Ganesha Tarot local app data को browser/OS/device protection से अलग अपनी encryption layer में encrypt नहीं करता। Device lock का उपयोग करें और ऐसी sensitive information न रखें जिसे unlocked device तक पहुँच रखने वाला व्यक्ति न देखे।

#### 6. Save, Share और screenshots

Save और Share user-initiated actions हैं। Save आपके डिवाइस पर image बना सकता है। Share exported image/text को OS, browser, share sheet या आपके चुने third-party destination तक पहुँचा सकता है। External destination चुनने के बाद उसकी privacy practices लागू होती हैं।

App ordinary screenshots को technically block नहीं करता। Screenshot आपके operating system/device द्वारा बनाया और संभाला जाता है। Personal, non-commercial screenshots Terms के अंतर्गत अनुमत हैं, लेकिन इससे protected assets के व्यापक redistribution rights नहीं मिलते।

#### 7. Voluntary support और PromptPay

PromptPay support screen QR और recipient identification information दिखाता है ताकि आप अपने banking app में voluntary transfer शुरू कर सकें। Little Ganesha Tarot bank password, PIN, OTP, card data या bank login credentials नहीं माँगता/लेता।

Payment app के बाहर bank/payment infrastructure द्वारा process होता है। Current app payment success independently receive या verify नहीं करता। Transfer authorise करने से पहले banking app में recipient name और amount जाँचें।

#### 8. Hosting, fonts और ordinary connection data

Profile/reading/Journal data local-first होने के बावजूद web/PWA use ordinary network requests बनाता है। Hosting provider और network intermediaries delivery के लिए IP address, timestamp, requested files और browser/device information जैसी standard technical request data देख सकते हैं।

Current UI Google Fonts infrastructure से fonts request करता है। Google या उसके service providers अपनी privacy terms के तहत ordinary connection/request metadata प्राप्त कर सकते हैं। Benedict Interactive इन requests से behavioural profile नहीं बनाता।

#### 9. Analytics, advertising और data sale

Current release में Benedict Interactive behavioural-advertising SDK या product analytics SDK नहीं है। Benedict Interactive इस release में personal profile, Journal या reading data advertisers को नहीं बेचता।

Future analytics, cloud sync, account, crash reporting या remote-data feature शुरू होने पर उसका data flow बताने के लिए Policy update होना जरूरी है।

#### 10. Retention और deletion

Functional reading records applicable daily rule की जरूरत तक या browser storage हटने तक रह सकते हैं। Profile/preferences और Journal entries आपके बदलने/delete करने तक local रह सकते हैं; वास्तविक retention browser/OS storage policies से भी प्रभावित हो सकती है।

Settings/Journal के deletion controls का उपयोग करें। Browser/OS स्तर पर site storage clear करने से अतिरिक्त local app data हट सकता है।

#### 11. Security और sensible use

Remote collection कम करने के बावजूद कोई device, browser, storage या network absolutely secure होने की guarantee नहीं देता। Device/browser updated रखें, device access सुरक्षित करें और tarot questions/reflections में passwords, banking credentials, government identifiers, medical records या अत्यधिक संवेदनशील secrets न लिखें।

#### 12. Children और legal capacity

Little Ganesha Tarot बच्चों का personal data solicit करने के लिए design नहीं किया गया। अगर आपके jurisdiction में आप स्वयं consent/contract की कानूनी क्षमता नहीं रखते, तो जहाँ कानून माँगे वहाँ parent/legal guardian की भागीदारी के साथ app use करें।

Optional profile date of birth legal age-verification system नहीं है, क्योंकि current product local-first है और account-based age verification नहीं करता।

#### 13. International use और mandatory rights

App कई देशों से इस्तेमाल किया जा सकता है और privacy/consumer/data-protection rights jurisdiction के अनुसार बदल सकते हैं। यह Policy किसी non-waivable legal right को हटाने के लिए नहीं है।

#### 14. Changes और contact

Policy legal version और update date दिखाती है। Remote collection/sharing/processing में material change होने पर updated policy, और जहाँ कानून माँगे वहाँ additional notice/consent, दिया जाएगा।

Privacy, licensing या legal questions official Benedict Interactive contact channel उपलब्ध होने पर वहीं भेजे जा सकते हैं। किसी unofficial social account को legal contact न मानें जब तक उसे officially identify न किया जाए।
