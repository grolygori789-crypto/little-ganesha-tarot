# Hindi Localization Standard V1

**Product:** Little Ganesha Tarot — The Golden Path  
**Language:** Hindi (हिन्दी)  
**Locale target:** Modern Indian Hindi (`hi-IN`)  
**Status:** V0.8.0 launch standard

## 1. Product voice

Hindi must feel originally written in Hindi, not translated from English or Thai. The voice is
warm, perceptive, calm and professionally experienced. Use respectful `आप`. Prefer natural modern
Hindi over stiff, ceremonial or unnecessarily Sanskritized wording. Familiar product/tarot loanwords
such as कार्ड, रीडिंग, फोकस, प्रोफ़ाइल and ऐप are acceptable when they are more natural than forced
alternatives.

## 2. Tarot voice

A reading should sound like an experienced reader interpreting the actual situation. Preferred flow:
context → what this card means here → why it matters → likely direction → caution/tension → practical
next step. Do not write card-dictionary definitions, generic horoscope prose, mystical filler or
absolute predictions.

The question/Focus owns the subject. The card cannot drag the answer away from that subject.
Contradictory cards are meaningful tension, not a reason to force artificial agreement.

## 3. Safety language

- Money: no guaranteed returns, price targets or investment instructions from tarot.
- Well-being: no diagnosis, pregnancy/recovery prediction or treatment claims.
- Legal: no verdict prediction.
- Gambling: no lottery, betting or winning-number prediction.
- Death: no death timing.
- Spiritual/unseen: symbolic reflection only; no claim that deities, spirits, curses or unseen forces
  have been objectively verified.
- Immediate safety/crisis language must not be routed into a tarot answer.

## 4. Typography

Use `Noto Sans Devanagari` for UI and `Noto Serif Devanagari` for editorial reading copy. Devanagari
requires comfortable line height and must not be vertically cramped. English canonical tarot titles
may remain as quiet secondary labels beneath the Hindi card title.

## 5. Coverage gate

Hindi is release-complete only when the user can move from title screen through every playable
reading mode, same-day restore, Focus status, countdown, Save/Share and settings without falling back
to visible English except proper names, brand names or deliberate canonical card subtitles.

## Ask Ganesha same-day semantics

Natural Hindi paraphrases that resolve to the same topic contract should restore the same same-day reading. Named-person relationship questions must keep different people separate. Exact and semantic restore must never reroll a locked same-day question.
