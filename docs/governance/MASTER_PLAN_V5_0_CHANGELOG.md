# Master Plan V5.0 — Accepted Worldwide Support Amendment

**Accepted by Founder:** P’Benz  
**Date:** 24 August 2026  
**Effective runtime:** V0.16.0

## Decision

Worldwide voluntary support is now **Ko-fi**, not Buy Me a Coffee.

Official destination:

`https://ko-fi.com/benedictinteractive`

This accepted decision supersedes every earlier V5.0 statement that names Buy Me a Coffee as the planned/current worldwide support provider.

All unchanged V5.0 support constraints remain binding:

- Open Access + Voluntary Support;
- support never affects tarot/Lucky/Journal or access;
- no entitlement;
- no custom payment backend;
- no account in Little Ganesha;
- no payment callback requirement;
- provider failure isolated from core app;
- EN / TH / HI native copy;
- external user-initiated navigation.

PromptPay remains the active Thailand route.

## Sequence amendment

The previous sequence:

`BMC activation → full QA → release decision`

becomes:

`Ko-fi activation → full QA → release decision`

V1.0.0 remains reserved for the first market-ready stable release after the complete release gate passes.
