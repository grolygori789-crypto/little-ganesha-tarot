# Reading Hub Product Standard V1

The Reading Hub is a premium chooser for existing tarot readings, not a sixth tarot spread.

## Product role

- Home = whole-app dashboard.
- Read = dedicated reading chooser.
- Tarot Library = learn and explore.
- Journal = private reflection archive.
- Settings = preferences and app management.

The Hub may recommend a mode from an explicit user-selected intent, but may not infer hidden psychological states, draw cards, alter probability, or claim supernatural matching.

## Intent map

1. Quick reflection → Daily Guidance.
2. One clear question → Ask Ganesha.
3. See how a situation is unfolding → Three-Card Reading.
4. Need direction → The Golden Path.
5. Something feels stuck → Remove the Obstacle.

Recommendation is reversible, optional and never auto-launches a reading.

## State display

Read-only status may be derived from existing local stores. The Hub must not write, clear, migrate or reset protected reading state. Deleting Journal data must remain unrelated to Hub status.

## Localization

EN / TH / HI are independently authored. Brand masthead remains LITTLE GANESHA TAROT / THE GOLDEN PATH.
