- Enlarges the Daily Guidance reveal card so the artwork has stronger visual priority.

- Rewrites/audits English and Thai keywords, meanings, and reflection prompts across all 78 cards.

REV3 extends the language audit beyond the Reading screen to the current application shell:

- Title/entry accessibility copy
- Onboarding
- Home
- Settings
- Profile
- Audio controls
- Support placeholders
- Navigation
- errors/helper text
- accessibility labels

Thai wording is optimized for natural contemporary Thai rather than literal English structure. English is optimized for clear international native usage rather than polished-but-synthetic product prose.
